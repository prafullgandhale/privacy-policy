Privacy Policy

App Name: Flavor Flow: Fruit Slicer

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

This policy discloses how Flavor Flow: Fruit Slicer ("the app") accesses, collects, uses, and shares user data.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require registration and does not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

  • Local Game Data: High scores and audio/vibration settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Age Gate Response: On first launch, you're asked your birth year to determine child-directed ad treatment. This is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Advertising Data: For users not identified as children, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may access device/advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. For users identified as children, ads are non-personalized and no advertising identifier is used.
  • Diagnostic Data: Google Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics collect crash logs and basic device/app performance data from all users, used only to fix bugs and maintain app stability.

3. Children's Privacy

This app uses a neutral age gate to apply child-directed treatment in line with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA. Users who indicate they are under 13 are served non-personalized ads only, with no advertising identifier or behavioral data used. We do not knowingly collect directly identifying information from children.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored app data at any time via Settings > Apps > Flavor Flow > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
